Which money-flow patterns should the AI detect?

FOIA Fluent uses 7 pattern types. For funding, these are the candidates. Select all that should ship in the MVP — each becomes a pattern_type in the schema, a branch in the Claude detection prompt, and a cluster-highlight style in the galaxy. Multi-select.

1

Sector Concentration

A sector funnels a disproportionate share of donations into a narrow set of politicians — often committee members who oversee them.

Example: "Defense contractors directed 78% of 2024 PAC donations to 12 Armed Services Committee members. Lockheed, Raytheon, and Northrop donated 3.4x their baseline rate to Chairman Rogers and ranking member Reed."

2

Dark Money Pathway ★ core

Funds routed through 501(c)(4)s before landing with politicians — the money enters the system with named donors attached, but the path obscures who those donors are.

Example: "Americans for Prosperity (501c4) transferred $14.2M from undisclosed donors to 8 Super PACs in Q3 2024 — all supporting 4 senators voting on SEC crypto regulations."

3

Committee Alignment

Industry PACs converge on members of the specific congressional committee that regulates their industry — above statistical baseline.

Example: "Pharma PACs donated to HELP Committee members at 4.2x the rate of non-members. PhRMA, Pfizer PAC, and Merck PAC combined: $8.7M to 21 members."

4

Sudden Surge

Cycle-over-cycle spike in donations from a sector — usually correlated with pending legislation or regulatory decisions.

Example: "Crypto industry donations up 340% vs. 2022 cycle, concentrating around 6 SEC-overseeing senators. Coinbase PAC alone tripled its spend."

5

Cross-Party Hedging

Same employer donates to both parties' leadership in the same cycle — often in races where specific legislation is at stake.

Example: "Blackstone donors split $2.3M 52/48 between R and D leadership PACs — every recipient sits on a committee overseeing private equity tax policy."

6

Geographic Anomaly

Out-of-state PACs concentrating in specific races where their industry has legislative exposure — regardless of candidate's home state.

Example: "Texas energy PACs concentrated in 3 non-Texas Senate races — all targeting candidates voting on federal pipeline permitting."

Recommendation

MVP: 1, 2, 3, 4 (Concentration, Dark Money, Committee Alignment, Sudden Surge). These four cover the most impactful stories, reuse data you already have in Supabase, and don't require geo or cross-party voting-record joins. Cross-Party (5) and Geographic (6) are excellent v2 additions once the core ships.

Note on cost: Claude Sonnet cron analyzing 400 edges weekly ≈ $0.50/run. Daily cost if you pick 6 patterns ≈ $15/mo. Weekly cron probably sufficient for MVP.
