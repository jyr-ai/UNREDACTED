How large is the "entire universe"?

Trade-off: more nodes = more complete picture, but slower renders and visual noise. Rendering tech depends on this number.

A

Curated · 300-500 nodes ★ recommended for MVP

Top ~150 employers (by cycle $ volume) + their top ~200 PACs/501c4s + top ~150 politicians. Threshold: entities accounting for >$50k/cycle. SVG (D3-force) renders smoothly at 60fps.

Pros: Fast. Readable labels. Patterns stand out. Matches FOIA Fluent's density. Cons: Long-tail donors excluded — but they'd be pixels anyway.

B

Medium · 1,500-2,500 nodes

All entities with >$10k/cycle. SVG with level-of-detail tricks (hide labels on zoom out, virtual scrolling). Still interactive but noticeable lag on lower-end laptops during force simulation.

Pros: More complete. Cons: Visual noise. Tick rate drops. Need aggressive clustering/decimation.

C

Full · 10k+ nodes (Canvas/WebGL)

Every FEC-registered entity. Must switch from SVG to Canvas (regl/deck.gl) to keep usable frame rates. Deck.gl is already a dependency per CLAUDE.md.

Pros: "Complete" picture. Cons: Significant eng. cost. Label rendering hard. Most nodes are single pixels — no signal, just noise. Users can't see trees for the forest.

Recommendation

A — Curated 300-500 nodes. Matches FOIA Fluent's density (they cap around 400). The galaxy's job is to tell money-flow stories; a $500 donor to a state assembly race isn't a story. Users who want full FEC detail already have the existing leaderboard + Sankey. Galaxy is for signal. Plus: A is the only option we can confidently ship fast on SVG/D3.

The "sector cluster" and "employer cluster" views on the Money Flow tab would be smaller still (20-80 nodes each) — always SVG.
